DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION

GSS PARBUK-KANGKONG

Government Secondary School Parbuk, established in 1964, stands as a prominent educational institution in Arunachal Pradesh. Affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), the school has a rich history and a strong commitment to academic excellence and holistic development. Located in a picturesque setting, GSS Parbuk benefits from the natural beauty of its surroundings, fostering a conducive environment for learning. The school’s infrastructure is designed to support a diverse curriculum, offering students a range of subjects from sciences to humanities, along with extracurricular activities that promote physical, artistic, and social skills.

About Us

Welcome to Government Secondary School Parbuk

One of the defining features of GSS Parbuk is its dedicated faculty. Teachers at the school are not only educators but also mentors who guide students in their personal and academic journeys. They employ innovative teaching methods to engage students, ensuring that learning is both enjoyable and effective. The school’s community plays a vital role in its success. Parents and local stakeholders actively participate in school activities, creating a supportive network that enhances the educational experience. This collaboration fosters a sense of belonging and encourages students to excel in all areas of life. GSS Parbuk also emphasizes the importance of character building and values education. Through various programs and activities, students are encouraged to develop qualities such as integrity, responsibility, and respect. This focus on values prepares them to become responsible citizens who contribute positively to society. In conclusion, Government Secondary School Parbuk is not just an educational institution; it is a nurturing ground for future leaders and changemakers. With its commitment to excellence, dedicated faculty, and supportive community, GSS Parbuk continues to be a beacon of education in Arunachal Pradesh, inspiring generations of students to reach their ultimate potential.
